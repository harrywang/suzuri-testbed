# Inkstone care

Rinse with cool water, never soap. Dry face-down on cloth.

Linked from [[grinding-the-ink]] and [[reading-list]]. The sections below are the embed targets used by [[embed-rendering-check]].

## Daily rinse

Cool water and a soft cloth, immediately after grinding. Ink left to dry in the well sets into the stone and lifts the polish when it is finally scrubbed out.

## Deep clean

Once a season, a bean-curd poultice left overnight draws out ink that has worked into the grain. Never a wire brush, and never boiling water.

## Storage

Face-down on cloth, out of direct sun. A lidded box if the room is dry enough to crack lacquer.
